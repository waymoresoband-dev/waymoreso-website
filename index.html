★ ABOUT ★

Formed in London in late 2023, WAY MORE SO are a pop-punk / alternative-rock trio built on loud guitars, melodic hooks and the energy of the early 2000s. Their debut single, “Yeah, Yeah, You’re Right!”, introduced the band’s no-apologies sound; its official music video is featured on this site. Their second single, “Stuck”, was written by Tiz, finished in summer 2026 and recorded at SAE Institute London. Released on 2 October 2026, it arrived with a lyric video, and its official music video is coming soon.
